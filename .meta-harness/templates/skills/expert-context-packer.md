# Outcome-First Expert Context Packer

Use only when one domain or methodology judgment is necessary to choose or validate the nearest product action. Do not create an expert packet for optional review, status reconciliation, unchanged passed evidence, or a terminal `NO_BUILD` state.

Packet order:

```text
Product result: <observable user outcome>
Primary action under consideration: <one action>
Exact unresolved question: <one question>
Decision alternatives: <at least two concrete options>
Discriminator: <evidence that selects between them>
Current authority: <locked intent and owner boundary>
Relevant immutable evidence: <minimal excerpts>
Demonstrated consequence of error: <journey prevention, invalid conclusion, irreversible loss, or supported-platform unusability>
```

Exclude implementation instructions that bias the answer, broad repository history, stale status, speculative concerns, and evidence outside the decision surface.

The expert may return a decision or a demonstrated blocker. Their preference cannot reopen an unchanged passed gate, create a successor slice after closure, or override owner authority.
